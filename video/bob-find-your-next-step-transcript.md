## Text alternative (short)

The 90-second film follows Sam, a fictional person with a full Tuesday, through the installed B.O.B. Windows app:

1. Sam captures a stray thought and decides its type.
2. Sam accepts one previewed suggestion for the next action.
3. Sam quiets the screen with Overwhelmed mode and starts the task.
4. Sam saves a place to come back to.
5. Sam finds that place still there after closing and reopening the app.
