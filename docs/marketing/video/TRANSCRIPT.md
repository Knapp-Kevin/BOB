Video: [`bob-alpha-walkthrough.mp4`](bob-alpha-walkthrough.mp4) (60 seconds, silent, captions in [`bob-alpha-walkthrough.en.vtt`](bob-alpha-walkthrough.en.vtt)). Poster frame: [`bob-alpha-walkthrough-poster.png`](bob-alpha-walkthrough-poster.png).

The recording has no audio. Every item and message shown is fictional demo data.

## Short text alternative

A screen recording of the installed B.O.B. alpha app on Windows. The user captures a thought, classifies it, and accepts one previewed change that makes a task the next action. They start the task, ask B.O.B. to break it down, and save their place. After B.O.B. is closed and reopened, the task and the saved handoff are still there. It ends on Settings, where optional inference providers are off and accessibility options are available.

## Limitations shown or implied

- Recorded from the installed alpha NSIS package inside a disposable Windows Sandbox with networking off. The package is unsigned and not a public release.
- The Chat replies come from B.O.B.'s local, rule-based guidance; no AI model was used.
- The video has one cut, at 0:43, where the app was closed and reopened.
